## Read before treating these as final

- **Taigun and Virtus** are the real India-market car, GT trim, so these are
  accurate representations of what the dealership sells.
- **Tiguan R-Line, Golf GTI and Tayron R-Line** are the correct model and
  generation, but the photographed car is not confirmed to be the exact
  India-market unit (colour, trim package or CBU-specific detailing may
  differ slightly from what ships here). Treat these three as a strong
  placeholder upgrade over the vector illustration, not a final asset. Swap
  them for dealer-supplied India-spec photography when it becomes available,
  by replacing the same filename in `public/models/`.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA both require attribution; CC BY-SA also requires that
  adaptations (a cropped or recoloured version) carry the same license. Keep
  this file in the repo and surface a credit line on the site (a small
  "Photo credits" link in the footer, or per-image caption, satisfies this).
